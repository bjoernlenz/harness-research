005 Trust Models

Trust is already emerging as a comparative dimension, even before it is fully normalized.

Patterns

  • Trust through closeness: Aider, Claude Code, Pi
  • Trust through explicit structure: LangGraph, PydanticAI, OpenAI Agents SDK, Semantic Kernel
  • Trust through supervision and environment design: Cursor, OpenHands
  • Trust through data grounding: LlamaIndex
  • Trust through team structure and termination: AutoGen

  • Explicit state: LangGraph

Blurry Distinctions

  • Semantic Kernel trusts through composition layers rather than visible agent behavior, which may be harder to feel immediately
