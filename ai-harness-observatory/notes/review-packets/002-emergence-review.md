## Most Important Hidden Bets

- Repo proximity matters more than orchestration sophistication
- Explicit state graphs outperform opaque autonomous loops
- Tracing and handoffs make multi-agent systems operationally manageable
- A small extensible core outcompetes giant integrated runtimes
- Data grounding is the true center of useful agency

## Top 5 Unresolved Contrasts

- Repo-close vs graph-close agency
- Trust through closeness vs trust through structure
- Shell worldview vs runtime worldview
- Minimal-core extensibility vs full embodied tooling
- Data-close vs orchestration-close agency
