# Data As Cognitive Substrate

Some harnesses assume the real problem is not how to make an agent more agentic. It is how to make the world it thinks over more structured, retrievable, and relevant. Data-as-cognitive-substrate names that wager.

LlamaIndex carries this most clearly in the current corpus. Its center of gravity is not the romantic image of an autonomous worker moving through tools. Its center is the preparation of an information terrain the system can traverse usefully. The index, the workflow, and the retrieval surface become the actual cognitive body.

There is a human story here too. These systems grow out of frustration with generic model cleverness that is untethered from the right knowledge at the right time. The emotional driver is less “give the model a personality” and more “stop letting it be ignorant in preventable ways.”

What this makes easier to see is why data-centered harnesses often feel powerful in enterprise and knowledge-heavy settings. They reframe intelligence as quality of grounding. What they can obscure is that better information terrain does not automatically produce better judgment, coordination, or planning. A well-fed agent can still be a weak worker.
